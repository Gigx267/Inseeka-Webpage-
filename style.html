Our Story

Hi, I'm Nsikan; creative entrepreneur and founder of Styled By Inseekan.

Styled By Inseekan was born from a simple but powerful purpose: showing up for my guys on their most important days. Naturally, I help men look sharp, feel confident, and stay calm, even with the pressure that comes with these big days, so that even though there might be hassles, your style and swagger will never be another thing to stress about. From weddings and milestone celebrations to moments that truly matter, I work closely with you to curate looks that feel effortless, intentional, and completely you.

Think of me as your fashion guardian angel, guiding you every step of the way, handling the details, and making sure you show up as your best, most confident self.

BOOKING POLICY

SECURING YOUR DATE

Bookings are confirmed subject to availability. A 25% non-refundable commitment fee is required to reserve your spot on our calendar.

Full payment must be received before commencement of work. Payment plans can be discussed during consultation.

TRAVEL & LOGISTICS

For events outside Abuja, travel and accommodation costs are not included in the package and will be billed separately.

For the best results, bookings should be made at least 15 - 18 weeks before your event. Rush bookings may attract extra fees.
